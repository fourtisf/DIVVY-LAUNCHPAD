# "The dev buys first": post copy for X

Images: `dev-buys-first-3200x1800.png` (16:9) or `dev-buys-first-2160x2700.png` (4:5). Renderer:
`brand/uspad/src/devbuy.cjs`. The coin in them is an example, $YOURCOIN. Every claim matches the site
since 2026-10-28 (owner decision): the dev buy rides inside the create (docs/VERIFIED.md item 29), from
0.01 to 80 SOL, quick picks 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1 SOL and Max, and the coin page shows "Dev bought X%".
1 SOL is about 3.4% of the supply on the empty curve. Each post is under 280 characters as X counts them.

## Post

```text
New on uspad: the dev buys first. 🟢

Your dev buy goes inside the create, in the same transaction, so it's your coin's first trade. Nobody can buy before it.

0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1 SOL or Max. Every coin shows how much its dev bought.

Launch → uspad.fun
```
