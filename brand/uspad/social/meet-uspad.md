> Every trade on a uspad coin pays a 1.25% fee:
>
> • 0.50% to holders
> • 0.15% to the creator
> • 0.35% to uspad
> • 0.25% to Raydium
>
> Every hour, the holders' share buys the coin's stock token and sends it to every wallet holding $10 or more, in proportion to its bag.

**3/**
> Fair from the first block.
>
> Every coin is created in a private Jito bundle, so nobody can snipe it. No dev buy, no team allocation.
>
> Your own wallet signs the create, so the coin's creator on chain is you.

**4/**
> At 85 SOL a coin graduates to a Raydium pool.
>
> Its liquidity is locked forever with Raydium Burn & Earn: nobody can pull it, uspad included. The locked liquidity keeps earning fees, and those keep paying holders.

**5/**
> The dividends are real tokenized stocks (xStocks), not points.
>
> Every hourly round is a public receipt on uspad.fun, so anyone can check what was paid and to whom.
