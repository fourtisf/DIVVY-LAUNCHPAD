## Image description (alt text)

```text
How uspad works: from a launch to stock dividends, every hour. 1, Launch: pick a name, a ticker and the stock your holders earn. Your wallet signs the create, sent in a private bundle. No snipers, no dev buy. 2, Trade: anyone buys and sells with SOL on the bonding curve, and every trade pays a 1.25% fee. 3, Buy the stock: every hour, the holders' 0.50% of every trade buys the stock token the creator picked, like NVDAx, SPYx or TSLAx. 4, Paid every hour: the stock goes to every wallet holding $10 or more of the coin, in proportion to what it holds, with on-chain receipts. Every trade's 1.25% is split four ways: 0.50% to holders in stock, 0.35% to uspad, 0.25% to Raydium and 0.15% to the creator. At 85 SOL a coin graduates to Raydium, its liquidity locked for good.
```
